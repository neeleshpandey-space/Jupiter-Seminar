# The Jupiter System

A Master's seminar exploring Jupiter as a natural laboratory for planetary formation, atmospheric dynamics, magnetospheric physics, and Solar System evolution.

## Overview

This repository contains my seminar presentation for the M.Sc. Planetary Sciences and Space Exploration program at Freie Universität Berlin.

The seminar focuses on how modern observations from spacecraft missions and telescopes have transformed our understanding of Jupiter's interior, atmosphere, magnetic field, moons, and role in shaping the Solar System.

## Topics Covered

### Formation and Migration

* Core accretion and gas giant formation
* Pebble accretion and runaway gas capture
* Grand Tack migration model
* Influence on terrestrial planet formation

### Atmospheric Dynamics

* Atmospheric structure and cloud layers
* Great Red Spot dynamics
* Deep circulation and ammonia distribution
* Shoemaker–Levy 9 atmospheric observations

### Interior Structure

* Diluted ("fuzzy") core hypothesis
* Helium rain
* Differential rotation
* Interior evolution constrained by Juno gravity data

### Magnetosphere

* Dynamo generation in metallic hydrogen
* Auroral processes
* Plasma interactions with Io
* Radiation belts and magnetospheric dynamics

### Galilean Moons

* Tidal heating
* Europa's subsurface ocean
* Ganymede's intrinsic magnetic field
* Habitability of icy worlds

### Small Bodies and Solar System Architecture

* Jupiter Trojans
* Ring system
* Long-term influence on Solar System evolution

## Missions Discussed

* Pioneer 10 & 11
* Voyager 1 & 2
* Galileo
* Cassini Flyby
* Juno
* JUICE

## Scientific Themes

* Giant planet formation
* Planetary atmospheres

* Comparative planetology
